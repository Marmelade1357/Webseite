01 About cat about.md

I’m Oualid from Bonn. By day I advise customers at Telekom. In the evenings and at weekends I build things.

A big part of that are board and card games I programmed for game nights with friends: with their own server, bots, reconnects and sometimes a 3D table. Everything runs in Docker containers on my Raspberry Pi. Right now I’m working on my first big game in Unreal Engine: Last Arrow.

My pattern is always the same: I come across a topic, read up quickly and then really sink my teeth in until something finished comes out of it. That goes for code as much as for AI, 3D printing or a cosplay I finish myself.

My Mandalorian cosplay is one of those cases: I printed and finished the entire armour myself. Some parts that didn’t exist the way I wanted, I designed myself, for example the grav charge holster. It’s available as my own model on MakerWorld to print yourself, and you can look at it in 3D right next to the photo.

02 Featured project man last-arrow

In development

Last Arrow

Defend your castle in a first-person shooter.

“Hold the castle as long as you can: one bow, one treasure, endless goblins, and after every boss your fortress grows.”

First-person castle defence. You stand in your castle courtyard with bow and arrows and hold off wave after wave of goblins trying to loot your treasure chest. After every boss your castle grows. Every run is endless, and the enemies keep getting tougher.

  • UE 5.8Unreal Engine 5.8 in C++
  • 165automated tests for game rules, weapons and systems
  • Server ↔ Clientthe server computes, clients display: clean separation of logic and presentation
  • Offlinefully offline: no account, no server, no internet needed
Trailer coming
mini-last-arrow.js Browser teaser, not the real game

Click or tap the goblins before they reach the chest. After every boss the castle grows.

How it plays

--bow
Draw, aim, hit weak spots for more damage.
--castle
Grows after every boss: more walls, more gates, walkable towers with stairs, bigger corner towers.
--walkable
Walk through your own gates, climb the walls, shoot from the towers.
--treasure
The chest is both target and bank account. If it falls, the run is over.

Weapons & economy

dealer
Buy, upgrade or sell weapons at the stall in the courtyard.
slots
Three weapon slots: the standard bow plus two bought weapons.
crossbow
Draw it with a visible string, load the bolt.
musket
Reload with the ramrod, knock goblins back with the recoil.
secret
An assault rifle with magazine and shell casings, only via a secret code. Meant as a gag.
cards
After every round, pick upgrades that change stats and effects.
goldmine
Build mines in the courtyard. The gold goes into new weapons and towers.
towers
Build and upgrade defence towers via the tower command.

Enemies

Animated goblins of several types, including runners from different directions and heavy enemies like the battering ram. Bosses unlock the next castle expansion. Hits have impact: enemies flinch, fall and get knocked over by the musket.

World

A warm, colourful fairy-tale world with sun, weather, forest, mountains and meadows. A courtyard with a house, market stall, flags, torches and gate banners. The castle, enemies and effects are almost all generated in the game itself. For the market stall, houses, chest, gold mine and bow I used AI-generated 3D models that I prepared for the engine.
